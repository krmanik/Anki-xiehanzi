# Features

### Multiple note types

The deck contains notes with several note types to practice meaning, pinyin or zhuyin, audio, tone marks and writing of characters. The card types are:

1. Learn meaning of characters
2. Learn Pinyin or Zhuyin of characters
3. Learn pronunciation using audio and Pinyin or Zhuyin
4. Learn tone marks of characters
5. Learn stroke order of characters

### Colored strokes

The stroke color matches the tone color of the first pinyin if multiple pinyin exist.

<img src="{base}/img/colored-stroke.gif" alt="Colored strokes" />

### Practice Simplified & Traditional characters

Easily toggle between simplified and traditional to practice both.

<img src="{base}/img/simplified-traditional.gif" alt="Simplified and traditional" />

### Pinyin & Zhuyin support

Both pinyin and zhuyin are available on every card.

### Audio

HSK 1 – HSK 9 audio is included in the decks.

### More-info sidebar

- View meaning with [Pleco dictionary](https://www.pleco.com/) on phone.
- View character details with [HanziCraft](https://hanzicraft.com/).
- View mnemonics with [rtega.be/chmn](http://rtega.be/chmn/).
- View meaning on [Youdao](http://dict.youdao.com).
- View stroke order and stories on [CharacterPop](https://characterpop.com).
- View example sentences on [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/en/).
